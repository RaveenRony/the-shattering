**A world once whole, now broken - and on the edge of unraveling again.**

Almost a millennium ago, the continent of **[[Elaria]]** shattered in a cataclysm known only as **The Shattering** - a world-splitting event that rained fire from the skies and tore the land into seven scattered isles. Now, the **Shattered Isles** stand as a fragmented remnant of a lost age, each island scarred by time, mystery, and creeping corruption.

This site acts an archive of the information and your journey through this world. Accessible here are the:
- Map of [[Elaria]] 
- Ongoing events in the world and [[The Story So Far]]
- Information from Jeff and Zarkon in their best-seller "History of Valtorra"

This is the wiki of the world that the campaign 1 - **The Shattering** is taking place in.
