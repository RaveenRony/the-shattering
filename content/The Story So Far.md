The party moves to Nor Grutta tribe where they are intercepted by a group of Kog Zeguro soldiers who ask if they saw the convoy but Kroff who was taking the rest of the party in chains lie to them and say no. The soldiers leave them alone but only after they tie the rest of them tightly. The party reaches Nor Gurtta and manages to meet Vrask who talks to Kroff and finds out that the new comers are here to take down Ghor'Nak. After asking them to give him the artifact for 1,200 gp, he also offers to help the heist by providing them with the distraction if they can convince that they have a solid plan to steal it. They are also informed that Kroff's best friend will arrive in 2 days and could help them by being the inside man. He secretly informs Kroff to spy on the party and steal the artifact if they don't give the artifact back. 

The party then splits up as Jozeth, Bhardrus and Kroff goes back to the Penteveral while Astor, Varis and Ghostblade move to the Kog Zeguro to get an idea of the city's layout. Jozeth gives up his empathy toward Wammu as he asked for Zarkon's plans, the Sphinx predicts that he wants to bring back the lost tribe and rewrite the world to a point where he is ruling the world. Kroff allows the memories of his parents to be removed from the world so that he could know why he was called the Misguided, the Sphinx reveals that he has another question for what he sacrificed but answers his question by letting him know that his father isn't who he thinks he is. The rest of the party manages to recon the entire city with Ghostblade getting an idea of Ghor'Nak's routine, while Astor recons the lands near the fortress walls while Varis finds out where the Horn is stored and the layout of the meeting place for the Cult. 

In the next 2 days, the layout recon continued while Jozeth dressed up as a Cultist managed to learn a bunch about the mannerisms of the tribesman and got some gear to wear. Kroff met up with Ilya, the bard who had promised him that he would help Kroff and his friends into the tent, if they were planning to kill Warlord Ghor'Nak. Kroff mentioned that he was going to steal the horn as well but Ilya only hesitated for a second before continuing to agree to help his best friend.

For the next 2 days, the party prepares for the heist with Astor charming the people around the city walls to ensure that they would be able to get some safehaven. Bhardrus prepares by training to be stealthy and nimble by stealthing around without his armour. Jozeth practices acting like Red Orc so he can get better with the act that he was going to put. Varis trained his magical awareness to be able to cast magic in quick way as well as feel the magical presence around him while Kroff trained his endurance to practice running away and causing a distraction and not get caught.
